IMPOSTER.

Pass the phone. Find the imposter.

How to Play

  1. Set the number of players and imposters, then hit Start.
  2. Pass the phone around — each player privately taps to reveal their role. Everyone gets the secret word except the imposter(s).
  3. Set the phone down. Take turns describing the word without saying it — the imposter has to bluff.
  4. When time's up (or anyone calls it), vote for who you think the imposter is.
  5. If the imposter is caught, they get one last chance to guess the word and steal the win.
